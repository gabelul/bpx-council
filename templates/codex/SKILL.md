# bpx-council — AI advisor council

When you need a second opinion or want multiple AI models to weigh in on a decision:

```bash
bpx-council "Is this approach sound?"
bpx-council --mode council "Architecture: monolith or microservices?"
```

Use before committing to architecture decisions, when stuck, or before declaring done.
